Adjectives vs Adverbs

Choose the correct form for each sentence.

Quick rule:
Use an adjective to describe a noun or after a linking verb (be, feel, seem, look).
Use an adverb to modify a verb, an adjective, or another adverb — often ends in -ly.

She is careful. (adjective — describes "she")  |  She drives carefully. (adverb — modifies the verb "drives")
