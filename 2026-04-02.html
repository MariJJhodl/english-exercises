5 exercises · Grammar, Vocabulary & Pronunciation

1

"Comfort" or "Comfortable"?

comfort → noun (conforto) or verb (consolare)
comfortable → adjective only (comodo / a proprio agio)
Quick test: can you replace it with cosy or at ease? → comfortable. Can you replace it with consolation or to console? → comfort.
2

Adjective or Adverb?

Use an adjective to describe a noun, or after a linking verb (be, feel, seem, look, smell, taste).
Use an adverb to modify a verb, an adjective, or another adverb — often ends in -ly.
She is careful.  |  She drives carefully.
3

Verb + Preposition

In English, many verbs are followed by a fixed preposition that cannot be translated literally from Italian.
Common traps: pensare a → think about (not "think to")  ·  guardare → look at (not "look to")  ·  arrivare a → arrive at / in (not "arrive to")

5

Pronunciation — Read Aloud

Read each sentence out loud. Before you start, look up the pronunciation of every highlighted word on wordreference.com — type the word in the search bar and click the speaker icon to hear it.

Pay special attention to:
• -ed endings — three different sounds: /t/ (worked), /d/ (called), /ɪd/ (visited)
• stress and syllables in longer words — English stress is very different from Italian
past simple -ed verbs
tricky pronunciation words

Part A  ·  Past simple -ed

She washed the dishes, packed her bag, and walked to the station. (-ed = /t/)
He called his sister, opened the door, and listened to her story. (-ed = /d/)
They visited a new city, wanted to see everything, and needed a map. (-ed = /ɪd/ — adds a syllable!)
She worked all morning, arrived late, waited for an hour, and finally talked to the manager. (mixed: /t/ /d/ /ɪd/ /t/)
He asked for help, decided to change his plan, and finished the project on time. (mixed: /t/ /ɪd/ /t/)

Part B  ·  Tricky words

The beneficiary of the insurance policy was directly contacted by the bank.
She felt frustrated because no one answered her questions directly.
The psychological effects of stress are often underestimated.
The doctor specifically recommended a psychological assessment.
The beneficiary particularly appreciated the direct communication.
He was thoroughly frustrated and decided to address the issue directly.
