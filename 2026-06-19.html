5 exercises · Grammar & Vocabulary

1

Verb & Adjective + Preposition

Many verbs and adjectives are always followed by a fixed preposition — you cannot translate directly from Italian.

New pairs to know today:
• proud of  •  tired of  •  accuse (sb) of
• deal with  •  insist on  •  succeed in
• blame (sb) for  •  suitable for
2

Verb + to-infinitive or -ing?

Some verbs change meaning depending on whether they take to + infinitive or -ing. The most important case is stop:

• stop -ing = cease an activity: “She stopped talking.” (she no longer talked)
• stop to + infinitive = pause in order to do something else: “She stopped to talk to him.” (she paused what she was doing in order to speak)

Other verbs with a fixed form today:
• to + infinitive: attempt, afford, tend
• -ing: risk, admit, imagine
3

Phrasal Verbs

The same verbs as last time — new sentences. Focus on using context to choose the right one.

• give off = emit (smell, heat, smoke, light)  •  give in = surrender, stop resisting
• give away = reveal a secret / give for free  •  run out of = have no more of something
• take over = assume control  •  come across = find or encounter by chance
• come across as = give the impression of being  •  set up = create, establish
• make up = invent a story / reconcile after an argument
4

Past Simple vs Past Perfect

Use the Past Perfect (had + past participle) when one past action happened before another past action.

“When she arrived, he had already left.”  →  he left first, then she arrived.

Use Past Simple for:
• Events in chronological order: “She finished the report and sent it.”
• After as soon as when both events happen in quick sequence: “As soon as the results were announced, they celebrated.”

Key signal words for Past Perfect: already, by the time, before, after, when (if one event clearly precedes the other)
5

Choose the Formal Word

Formal writing avoids colloquial verbs and informal expressions. Each question shows a colloquial sentence (in italics), followed by a formal rewrite with one gap.

New pairs today:
• look into → investigate  •  think about → consider  •  need → require
• cut down on → reduce  •  take part in → participate in  •  say → state
• buy → purchase  •  follow (rules) → comply with
